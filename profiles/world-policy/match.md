# World, politics and public policy profile

Use this profile when an item's primary value is understanding public affairs rather than a technology, a market price, or a company.

Typical items include elections and leadership changes, legislation, court rulings and constitutional disputes, wars, diplomacy, treaties and sanctions, international organisations, geopolitical realignment, migration and border policy, civil liberties, and social policy such as education, healthcare, pensions, housing, employment, welfare, and the distributional consequences of government decisions.

Route items whose main subject is a market move, a central bank rate decision, company earnings, financing, or valuations to the financial news profile. Route product releases, research results, software, and engineering developments to the technology news profile.

Do not route routine political process without consequence (horse-race polling, campaign gossip, ceremonial events, partisan talking points without substance), crime stories without policy significance, or opinion pieces that offer no factual development.
